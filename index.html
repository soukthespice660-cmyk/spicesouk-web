Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Pick your dishes from the menu and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. A message with your order opens; review it and send it. The order is confirmed once we reply.

How far in advance should I order?

At least 24 hours ahead. Everything is prepared to order.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, €25 for delivery and pickup. The delivery fee is not included.

Where do you deliver?

Central Madrid and surroundings, or you can pick up your order. Area, delivery fee and time are confirmed on WhatsApp.
